Trending templates FAQ

Browse all templates
  • Are the templates free?

    Yes. Viggle gives you free credits that refresh daily, and no credit card is needed to start. Free videos carry a small watermark; paid plans remove it.

  • How often do new trends show up here?

    We add a template here when it takes off on Viggle. The app gets new templates every day, so the newest ones are always there first.

  • How do I hear about new trending templates?

    Sign up for Viggle and turn on product emails to get a short weekly email, "This week on Viggle", with the templates worth trying. You can turn it off from any email. This page is updated when a template takes off, too.

  • Can I use my pet or a cartoon character?

    Yes. Any character with a clear full body works: pets, anime characters, 3D renders, mascots and drawings.

  • Do the templates work on my phone?

    Yes. The templates work in the Viggle app on iPhone and Android and on the web at viggle.ai.
