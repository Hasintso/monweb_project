FAQ

How do I make a meme?

Pick a template on this page or upload your own image. Type the top and bottom text, drag it where you want it, and change the size if it does not fit. Then download the PNG or copy it straight into a chat. It is free and you do not need an account.

How do I make a GIF or video meme?

Pick one of the moving meme templates, change the text if you like, then choose Video or GIF and download. The video keeps the original sound; the GIF is silent and plays anywhere, including group chats. Each clip is about three seconds.

Are the images I upload stored anywhere?

No. The meme is drawn in your browser, so your image never leaves your device. A picture is only uploaded if you choose to turn it into a video in the Viggle app.

How to make a good meme?

A great meme is simple, relatable, and taps into current trends or popular topics. Use minimal text and a striking image or video to connect with a broad audience. With Viggle, you can create a standout meme by starting with our collection of already-viral templates.

Why use an AI meme generator?

An AI meme generator makes creating memes faster, easier, and more customizable. With AI tools like Viggle, you can instantly add unique features—such as body swaps, lip-sync, and animation—transforming static images into dynamic, personalized memes.

Why use Viggle to create memes?

Viggle offers a truly immersive and personalized meme-making experience by letting you place yourself directly into meme videos. As the hottest AI meme generator, we’re known for viral hits like Lil Yachty’s best walkout and the "Gimme Head Top" dance swap, making it easy for your creations to go viral on social.

Can I upload my own meme template?

Absolutely! You're welcome to use your own video as a meme template. Feel free to share it in the Viggle app content feed so others can remix your template—and you can rack up thousands of likes!

How do I put myself in a meme video?

Upload a photo of yourself into Viggle's Mix tool, pick a meme template from the library of 10,000+ free templates, and the AI swaps you into the video in place of the original person. A full body photo works best because the model needs to see your head, torso and legs to map the motion accurately. You can swap in more than one person, so you and a friend can appear in the same meme.

Is Viggle's AI meme generator free?

Yes. Viggle offers free credits that refresh daily and no credit card is required to start. The free tier is enough for casual meme making and sharing with friends. Paid plans unlock higher resolution exports, watermark free downloads, faster processing and higher daily limits for people posting regularly to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Can I turn an existing meme image into a video?

Yes. Viggle's Move tool animates a static meme image. Upload the meme picture, then add a motion video or record yourself performing the movement, and the AI transfers those expressions and body movements onto the image. This is how a classic still meme becomes a short video clip without any animation or editing skills.

What kind of photo works best for an AI meme generator?

A full body photo of one person, shot head to toe against a simple background with even lighting. Arms and legs should be visible and not overlapping the body. Cropped selfies and half body shots produce the weakest results because the AI has no reference for the lower body and has to guess the limbs, which is what causes bent or distorted legs in the output.

Can I post AI generated memes on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Viggle memes export in short form video formats built for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and group chats, and you can share straight from the app. Free plan exports are intended for personal sharing. If you are posting from a brand account or monetizing the content, check the plan you are on and Viggle's Terms of Use, and avoid templates built around copyrighted characters.

How to edit a video with Viggle Remake

Upload a video

Remake runs as a chat in the Viggle app. No green screen, no reshoot.

  1. 1 . Add your video and say what to change

  2. 2 . Check the plan

    In Ask first mode, Remake shows its plan and the price before anything renders: what it replaces and what it keeps. Change it in plain words, then start.

  3. 3 . Get your remake and revise it

    Watch the result, ask for another take or change one part, and switch between versions of the same remake.

Viggle Remake FAQ

  • What is video to video AI?

    Video to video AI changes what is in a video you already have, instead of generating a new clip from a prompt. Viggle Remake keeps the motion, timing, camera and sound and changes only what you describe.

  • What is an AI video agent?

    An AI video agent takes a goal in plain words and does the steps itself. Viggle Remake plans the edit, paints keyframes, renders and revises when you ask, in one chat.

  • How long can the video be?

    In the app, up to 5 minutes: Remake renders windows of about 15 seconds and keeps them consistent, and the longest example here runs 55 seconds.

  • Can it change a video's background without a green screen?

    Yes. Describe the new set or attach a photo, and Remake replaces the background in every frame while the performers and camera move stay.

  • What are Viggle Remake's limits?

    Chrome and glass look approximate, a hand sweeping across a face in a fast spin can briefly merge with it, small text on new objects isn't reliable, and output is 720p.

  • How does Viggle Remake compare to Runway Aleph?

    Aleph 2.0 edits up to 30 seconds at 1080p (runway.com/product/aleph-2, checked November 20, 2026). Remake takes up to 5 minutes, keeps several recast people apart, and revises in chat. Aleph outputs a higher resolution.

  • How does Viggle Remake compare to Higgsfield Genjutsu?

    Genjutsu takes 4–30 second clips with up to 30 reference images (higgsfield.ai/genjutsu, checked November 20, 2026). Remake takes up to 5 minutes, changes people, objects, sets, weather and style in one chat. Genjutsu outputs up to 1080p and accepts more reference images.

  • Is Viggle Remake free?

    No. Remake is included in every paid Viggle plan, from $7.99 a month billed yearly.

How to replace characters in a video

Upload a video

Remake runs as a chat in the Viggle app; with Ask first on, it shows the price before it starts.

  1. 1 . Upload the clip and describe each new character

    Drop in up to 5 minutes of video and say who becomes what, in words or with a photo each. Here the dancer with the yellow cloth becomes a red fox in a golden cape and the one with the red cloth a silver robot.

  2. 2 . Check the plan and the price

    Remake lists each replacement and what it must keep. With Ask first on, it adds the cost and waits for you; in Auto it starts right away.

  3. 3 . Review, revise and download

    The fox and the robot dance the original routine on the same stage. Ask for another take or change one character, then keep the best version.

Character swap video FAQ

Recast your first clip
  • What is AI character replacement?

    It swaps a person in existing footage for a different character while the motion, timing and camera stay. On Viggle, Mix swaps one person from a photo, Remake Flash up to five from a photo each, Multi-Track up to eight across scenes, and Remake recasts by chat, costumes and animals included.

  • How is a character swap different from a face swap?

    After a face swap the hair, body and clothes still belong to the person who was filmed; a character swap replaces the whole figure, so a dancer can become a fox or a character you designed. For the face alone, use Viggle’s face swap.

  • How do I replace several characters in a video at once?

    For people from photos, pick Remake Flash in Mix: one photo per person, up to five, and who each one replaces. For costumes or animals, tell Remake who becomes what, in words or with one image each.

  • What does it cost to replace characters in a video?

    Mix is free to start, with daily credits and a watermark on free videos. Remake Flash shows its price on the Generate button. Remake is in every paid plan from $7.99 a month billed yearly, with no fixed cost per run; with Ask first on, it shows each price before it renders.

  • Will the characters get mixed up when people cross or the camera cuts?

    Remake ties each character to one performer through turns and cuts, as the musketeers show. Give each one something only they have, such as a colour, a hat or a side of the frame.

  • How long a clip can I recast?

    Remake takes 5 seconds to 5 minutes at 720p and 24 fps with the original sound, landscape or 9:16; Remake Flash takes 5 to 15.

  • Remake Flash, Multi-Track or Remake: which one for several people?

    Remake Flash for up to five people from photos in clips up to 15 seconds. Multi-Track for up to 8 characters across scenes and camera shots, each from its own photo. Remake for costumes, mascots or animals described in words, in clips up to 5 minutes, with every version kept.
